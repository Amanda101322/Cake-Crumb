White Forest Cake

The white forest is a really appealing cake and fits its name. It's coated with white fudge and has fruits as decor. Apart from that, the taste is heavenly when baked well

Ingredients

  • Egg whites
  • Granulated Sugar (for meringue)
  • Egg yolks
  • All-purpose flour(sifted)
  • Whole Milk
  • Vanilla Bean/Extract
  • Cornstarch
  • Sheet Gelatin (softened in cold water)
  • White Chocolate(chopped)
  • Mascarpone Cheese
  • Heavy Whipping Cream(chilled)
  • Pitted Cherries in Brandy/Syrup
  • Whole Glacé/Fresh Cherries

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C and grease a deep cake pan
  2. Beat egg whites with sugar until firm peaks form
  3. Fold yolks into the meringue, then gently fold in the sifted flour
  4. Transfer to the prepared pan and bake for 45 minutes. Let cool completely
  5. In a hot skillet, saute pitted cherries with sugar
  6. Deglaze with cherry liqueur, add Kirsch/brandy, and carefully ignite to flambe
  7. Remove from heat and drain, reserving the juices for the soaking syrup
  8. Heat whole milk, vanilla, and 1/3 of the sugar in a saucepan
  9. In a bowl, whisk remaining sugar, egg yolks, flour, and cornstarch
  10. Temper the yolk mixture with hot milk, return to saucepan, bring to a boil, and cook for 2 minutes while whisking constantly
  11. Remove from heat and stir in softened gelatin, white chocolate, and mascarpone until smooth. Spread on a plastic-wrapped tray and chill
  12. Whip cold heavy cream to medium-firm peaks. Smooth out the chilled custard and gently fold in the whipped cream
  13. Boil water and sugar together in a small pot for syrup
  14. Remove from heat and mix in the cherry liqueur, brandy, and reserved juices from the flambeed cherries
  15. Place the bottom sponge layer down and generously brush with the cherry syrup
  16. Spread a layer of white chocolate diplomat cream and dot with flambeed cherries
  17. Repeat process for all layers
  18. Coat the entire cake with remaining diplomat cream
  19. Melt white chocolate to form decorative bark or shave it into curls, pressing shavings all around the cake

Prep time: 25-30 mins

Bake time: 45 mins

Vanilla Sponge Cake

A light, airy, and delicately sweet layer cake with pure vanilla flavor

Ingredients

For the vanilla sponge
  • 2 cups of cake flour(or all-purpose flour sifted with 2 tbsp of cornstrach removed)
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp of salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  • Granulated or caster sugar(1 cup)
  • 4 large eggs(at room temperature)
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract(or vanilla bean paste)
  • 1/4 whole milk(at room temperature)
For the Vanilla Buttercream Filling & Frosting
  • 1 cup unsalted softened butter
  • 3 1/2 cups of sifted powdered sugar
  • 3 tbsp heavy cream or whole milk
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350 farenheit
  2. Grease your cake pans, line the bottoms with parchment paper, and lightly flour the sides
  3. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt together in a medium bowl. Set aside
  4. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar on medium-high speed for 4-5 minutes until light, pale and fluffy
  5. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in the vanilla extract
  6. Divide the batter into your pans
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes until golden brown
  8. Cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto wire racks to cool completely
  9. Beat the buter on medium-high speed for 2 minutes until creamy
  10. Gradually add pwdered sugar, heavy cream, vanilla and salt. Then beat on low speed until combined, then increase to high speed for 1-2 minutes until light and fluffy
  11. Place one cooled sponge layer on a plate
  12. Spread a layer of buttercream(or optional fruit jam) over the top
  13. Top with the second sponge layer, then frost the top and sides as desired

Prep time: 20 minutes

Bake time: 25-30 minutes

Red Velvet Cake

A stunning vibrant red layer cake with a signature tang, topped with rich cream cheese frosting

Ingredients

For the cake batter
  • 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened natural cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1 cup of vegetable oil or canola oil
  • 2 large eggs(at room temperature)
  • 1 cup buttermilk(at room temperature)
  • 1 tbsp red gel food coloring
  • 1 pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp distilled white vinegar
For the cream cheese frosting
  • 16 oz full-fat cream cheese(softened)
  • 1/2 cup of softened unsalted butter
  • 4 cups of powdered sugar, sifted
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350 degree farenheit
  2. Grease two 9-inch round cake pans, line the bottoms with parchment paper, and lightly flour the sides
  3. In a medium bowl, sift together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt
  4. In a large bowl, whisk the sugar, and vegeable oil together. Add the eggs, buttermilk, red gel food coloring, and vanilla extract. Whisk until smooth and evenly colored
  5. Gradually fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined
  6. Add the vanilla extract and salt. Beat on high speed for 1 minute until smooth and creamy
  7. Place the first cooled cake layer flat-side down on your cake stand or plate
  8. Spread a generous layer of cream cheese frosting over the top
  9. Place the second layer on top and cover the top and sides of the cake with the remaining frosting

Prep time: 25-30 minutes

Black Forest Cake

A german dessert featuring sponge layers brushed with cherry liqueur syrup, filled with cherries and light whipped cream

Ingredients

For the Chocolate cake layers
  • All-purpose flour: 2 1/4 cups(270g)
  • Cocoa powder: 2 cups
  • Baking Powder: 1 1/2 teaspoons
  • Baking soda: 1 teaspoon
  • Salt: 1/2 teaspoon
  • Dutch-Processed Cocoa powder: 1/2 cup
  • Espresso powder: 2 tsp
  • Hot boiling water: 2/3 cup
  • Whole milk: 1/2 cup
  • Vegetable oil: 1/2 cup
  • Sour cream: 1/2 cup
  • Eggs(large): 2 whole
  • Vanilla Extract: 1 tbsp
For the macerated cherries & brush syrup
  • Fresh pitted cherries: 2 1/2cups
  • Granulated sugar: 1/4 cup
  • Kirsch liqueur(or cherry juice): 1/4 cup
For the whipped cream frosting
  • Cold heavy cream: 4 cups
  • Granulated sugar: 1/2 cup
  • Vanilla Extract: 2 tsp(0r 2 tbsp kirsch)
For decoration and optional ganache drip
  • Semi-sweet chocolate bar: 1 bar(for chocolate shavings and curls)
  • Whole cherries with stems: 8 whole
  • Hot heavy cream(for optional ganache): 80g

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 175 degree celcius
  2. Grease and line the bottoms of two 8-9nch round cake pans with parchment paper
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, granulated sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt
  4. In a second bowl, whisk cocoa powder and espresso powder with 2/3 cup boiling water until fully dissolved
  5. Whisk the whole milk, vegetable oil, sour cream, eggs and vanilla extract into the cocoa mixture
  6. Pour the wet cocoa mixture into the dry flour mixture and whisk until combined and smooth
  7. Divide batter evenly between prepared pans
  8. Bake for 30-35 minutes until the center springs back when lightly pressed
  9. Cool in pans for 20 minutes, then invert unto wire racks to cool completely
  10. Combine halved cherries, 1/4 cup sugar, and 1/3 cup Kirsch(or cherry juice) in a bowl. Let sit for 30-60 minutes
  11. Strain and reserve 2/3 cup of the released cherry-Kirssch liquid to use for brushing the cake
  12. Slice each cooled cake layer horizontally in half to create 4 thin layers
  13. Beat heavy cold cream and powdered sugar in a stand mixer on medium-high speed until soft peaks form
  14. Add vanilla extract(or kirsch) and continue whipping until firm, stiff peaks form
  15. Place the first cut cake layer on a plate and brush with 3 tbsp of the reserved cherry liquid
  16. Spread 3/4 cup whipped cream over the top and press 1/3 of the macerated cherries into the cream
  17. Repeat with the second and third cake layers, brushing each with liquid, adding whipped cream, and topping with cherries
  18. Place the fourth layer on top, brush with remaining liquid, and frost the top and sides with the remaining whipped cream

Prep time: 45 minutes

Bake time: 30-35 minutes

Chocolate Fudge Cake

Rich, moist chocolate sponge cake topped with silky dark chocolate ganache.

Ingredients

For the cake batter
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups of granulated sugar
  • Unsweetened dutch-processed cocoa powder
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs all at room temperature
  • 1 cup buttermilk(or milk mixed withh 1 tbsp lemon juice)
  • 1/2 cup of vegetable oil or melted coconut oil
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 cup of freshly brewed hot coffee(or 1 cup boiling water + 1 tsp)
For the fudge frosting
  • 1 cup of softened unsalted butter
  • 1 cup of unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 3 1/2 cups of powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cup of heavy cream(or whole mili)
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C)
  2. Grease your pans and line the bottoms with parchment paper rounds. Lightly dust the sides wiht cocoa powder
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt
  4. Add the eggs, buttermilk, oil, and vanilla extract. Beat on medium speed for about 2 minutes until fully combined and smooth
  5. Reduce speed to low and simply pour in the hot coffee. Mix until just combined
  6. Divide the batter evenly between the two prepared cake pans
  7. Bake for 3-035 minutes, or until the toothpick you'll insert comes out clean
  8. Let the cakes cool in the pans for 10-15 minutes, then turn them out onto racks to cool before completely frosting
  9. In a large mixing bowl, beat the softened butter on medium speed for 2 minutes until creamy
  10. Sift in the cocoa powder and powdered sugar alternatly withh small splashes of heavy cream, beating on low speed until incorporated
  11. Add the vanilla and pinch of salt. Beat on high speed for another 1-2 minutes until the frosting is thick, smooth and glossy
  12. Place the first cooled cake layer flat-side down on a serving plate or cake board
  13. Spread about 1 cup of frostig evenly over the top layer
  14. Place the second layer on top(flat-side up for a level top)
  15. Spread the remaining frosting over the top and side of the cake using an offset spatula

Prep time: 25-30 minutes

Bake time: 35-40 minutes

Strawberry Shortcake

A classic summer dessert featuring tender, buttery sweet biscuit scones split open and layered with juicy marcerated strawberries and freshly whipped cream

Ingredients

For the Macerated Strawberries
  • Fresh strawberries(hulled and sliced): 1 lb
  • Granulated sugar: 3 tbsp(40g)
  • Fresh Lemon juice: 1 tsp
For the whipped cream
  • Heavy whipping cream(cold): 1 cup
  • Powderedsugar: 2 tbsp
  • Pure vanilla extract: 1 tsp

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, toss the sliced strawberries with 3 tbsp granulated sugar and 1 tsp lemon juice
  2. Let stay at room temperature for atleast 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the berries release their natural juices and form a sweet syrup
  3. Preheat your oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper
  4. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, 1/4 cup sugar, baking powder, and salt
  5. Cut in the cold butter using a pastry cutter or your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs
  6. Stir in 3/4 cold heavy cream and vanilla extract until a shaggy dough forms.
  7. Turn dough onto a slightly floured surface, pat into a 3/4-inch-thick rectangle, and cut into 6-8 round rounds using a biscuit cutter
  8. Transfer to the baking sheet, brush tops with heavy cream, and sprinkle with extra sugar
  9. Bake for 15-18 minutes until golden brown. Cool on a wire rack for 10 minutes
  10. Beat cold heavy cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla extract on medium-high speed until soft-to-medium peaks form
  11. Spoon a generous portion of maceraated strawberries and syrup over the bottom half, top with a large dollop of whipped cream

Prep time: 25 minutes

Bake time: 15-18 minutes

Vanilla Swiss Cake Roll

Ultra-soft, melt-in-your-mouth vanilla chiffon cake roll filled with a smooth mascarpone whipped cream filling that rolls perfectly without cracking or peeling

Ingredients

For the Chiffon Cake Batter
  • Cooking oil(neutral): 60g/4.5 tbsp
  • Milk: 80g/1/3 cup
  • Low-gluten/cake flour(sifted): 100g/3/4cup
  • Eggs(large and seperated): 6 whole
  • Vanilla Extract: 1/4 tsp
  • Lemon Juice: 2g
  • Granulated sugar: 65g/5 tbsp
For the Mascarpone Cream Filling
  • Mascarpone Cheese: 100g
  • Granulated Sugar: 18g/1.5 tbsp
  • Vanilla Extract: 1/4 tsp
  • Heavy Cream/Whipping Cream: 120g /1/2 cup

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together 60g cooking oil and 80g milk until combined
  2. Sift in 100g low-gluten flour and whisk until smooth
  3. Seperate 6 eggs then add the egg yolks and 1/4 tsp vanilla extract to the batter, mixing well until smooth and glossy
  4. Add 2g lemon juice to the 6 egg whites
  5. Whip on medium-high speed until large bubbles form, then add 1/3 of the suggar(about 22g)
  6. Continue whipping until fine bubbles form, then add another 1/3 of the sugar
  7. when soft lines appear, add the remaining sugar, reduce speed to medium-low, and whip until medium-to-wet peaks form
  8. Fold 1/3 of the meringue into the yolk batter until incorporated
  9. Pour the batter back into the remaining meringue and fold gently withh a spatula until smooth and uniform
  10. Pour the batter into a 25times40 cm baking pan lined with parchment paper
  11. Smooth the surface using a spatula
  12. Bake tat 340°F for 35 minutes until lightly golden
  13. Remove from the oven and immediately cover the top with a piece of parchment paper to trap moisture and prevent drying
  14. In a bowl, blend 100g mascarpone cheese, 18g sugar, and 1/4 tsp vanilla extract until smooth
  15. Gradually pour in 120g heavy cream and whip until smooth, thick and spreadable
  16. Flip the cake sheet over and peel off the bottom baking liner
  17. Trim off three outer edges. slice one short edge at a 45 degree angle,and make 4 shallow parallel cuts across the opposite short edge
  18. Spread the mascarpone cream evenly across the top surface
  19. Using the parchment paper underneath and a rolling pin for leverage, tightly roll the cake starting from the scored end toward the angled end
  20. Wrap the roll firmly in parchment paper and refrigerate for 1 hour to set
  21. Unwrap, slice with a knife and serve

Prep time: 25 minutes

Bake time: 35 minutes

Tres Leches Cake

a light, airy sponge cake soaked in a three-milk mixture...simply baked to perfection

Ingredients

For the sponge cake
  • All-purpose flour: 2 cups
  • Vanilla extract: 2 tsp
  • Baking powder: 3/4 tsp
  • Granulated sugar: 1 1/4 cups
  • Salt: 1/2 tsp
  • Eggs(large, seperated): 5 whole
  • Granulated sugar: 1 1/4 cups
  • whole milk: 1/2 cup
For the Tres Leches Soak
  • Evaporated milk: 1 can
  • Whole milk: 2/3 cup
  • Sweetened condensed milk: 1 can
For the whipped cream topping
  • Heavy whipping cream(very cold): 2 cups
  • Powdered sugar: 2 tbsp
  • Fresh sliced strawberries(for decorating)

Instructions

  1. preheat oven to 350 farenheit
  2. lightly butter or spray a pan
  3. whisk flour, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl
  4. Seperate the 5 eggs. Beat the egg whites on high speed until foamy. Slowly sprinkle in 1/4 cup sugar while beating until stiff peaks form
  5. In a seperate bowl, beat the 5 egg yolks with the remaining 1 cup of sugar until thick and pale
  6. stir the yolk mixture into the dry flour mixture until just combined
  7. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the yolk-flhes so you don't deflate the air
  8. Pour the batter into the pan and level with a spatula
  9. Bake for 25-30 minutes until fully baked
  10. Whisk the evaporated milk, 2/3 cup whole milk, and sweetend condensed milk together until well combined
  11. Let the cake cool until warm. Poke holes all over it using a fork or skewer
  12. Slowly pour the tres leches mixture over the cake
  13. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour so the cake absorbs the milk liquid
  14. Beat cold heavy cream and powdered sugar until soft peaks form, finishing by hand to achieve soft billowy peaks
  15. Spread whipped cream evenly over the chilled cake
  16. Decorate with sliced fresh strawberries before slicing

Banana Cake

A simple, light and spongy single-layer banana cake

Ingredients

  • Granualted sugar: 1 cup
  • Unsalted butter(softened): 1/2 cup
  • All-purpose flour: 2 cups
  • Baking powder: 2 1/2 tsp
  • Eggs(large, room temperature): 2 whole
  • Salt: 1/2 tsp
  • Whole Milk: 1/4 cup + 1tbsp
  • Mashed ripe bananas: 1 cup

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) for 15 minutes
  2. Grease a cake pan and line with parchment paper
  3. In a mixing bowl, combine 1 cup granulated sugar and 1/2 cup softened butter
  4. Beat on low then medium spped until the mixture is light, pale, and fluffy
  5. Add 2 tsp vanilla extract and 1 egg. Beat on medium speed until creamy
  6. Add the second egg and beat again until smooth and well incorporated
  7. Place a fine-mesh seive over the bowl
  8. Add 2 cups flour, 2 1/2 tsp baking powder, and 1/2 tsp salt into the seive
  9. Mix dry ingredients in the seive and sift directly into the wet batter
  10. Mix gently on low speed
  11. Pour in 1/4 cup + 1 tbsp milk and mix on medium speed until smooth and lump-free
  12. Add 1 cup of mashed ripe bananas into the batter
  13. Mix briefly on low speed until combined(do not overmix)
  14. Pour batter into the prepared cake pan and level the surface. Tap the pan on the counter a few times to release air bubbles
  15. Bake at 170 degree celcius for 25-30 minutes until a skewer or toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
  16. Allow the cake ot cool in the pan for 10 minutes
  17. Invert onto a wire rack and allow to cool completely before eating

Prep time: 15 minutes

Bake time: 20-30 minutes

Carrot Cake

Ultra moist, perfectly spiced layer cake packed with fresh grated carrots and toasted pecans, finished wit a rich and creamy cheese frosting

  • Baking soda: 1 tsp
  • All-purpose flour: 2 1/2 cups
  • Ground cinnamon 1 tsp
  • Granulated sugar: 1 cup

For the cream cheese frosting

  • Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)

  • Peel fresh carrots and grate using a box grater to yeild 3 cups

Marble Cake

For the chocolate swirls
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder: 3 tbsp
  • Warm Milk or Hot Water: 3 tbsp

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 9-inch loaf pan

  3. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and granulated sugar for 3-4 minutes until light, pale and fluffy

Classic Honey Cake(Medovik)

A tender, multi-layered golden cake infused with caramelized honey, snadwiched between layers of tangy, sweetened sour cream frosting that melts together as it rests

Ingredients

For the honey layers
  • Unfiltered honey: 1/2 cup
  • Granulated Sugar: 3/4 cup
  • Unsalted butter: 1/2 cup
  • Baking Soda: 1 tsp
  • Eggs(large, room temperature): 3 whole
  • All-purpose flour: 3 1/2 cups
  • Salt: 1/4 tsp
For the sour cream frosting
  • Full-fat sour cream(cold): 3 cups
  • Heavy Whipping Cream(cold): 1 cup(240 ml)
  • Powdered sugar(sifted): 1 1/4 cups
  • Pure Vanilla Extract

    • Instructions

    1. In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine the honey, granulated sugar, and unsalted butter. Stir continuously until melted and smooth
    2. Bring to a low boil and cook for 2-3 minutes until the mixture turns a deep golden-amber color
    3. Remove from heat and stir in the baking gsoda. The mixture wil foam up significantly-whisk constantly for 1 minute until smooth, then let cool for 5 minutes
    4. Whisk the eggs and salt together in a small bowl
    5. Slowly pour the eggs into the warm honey mixture while constantly mixing to prevent curdling
    6. Gradually fold in the flur with a spatula until a soft dough forms
    7. Divide the dough into 8 equal portions. Keep them covered under plastic wrap so they don't dry out
    8. Then preheat your oven to 350°F
    9. Roll out one dough portion directly onto a piece of parchment paper until very thin(about 8-9 inches in diameter)
    10. Prick the rolled dough several times with a fork to prevent bubbling
    11. Bake for 3-4 minutes until golden brown
    12. Immediately remove the oven and 8-inch pan or cake ring to press down and cut a perfect circle. Save the outer scraps on a seperate tray to cool
    13. Repeat with the remaining 7 dough portions. Cool all rounds completely
    14. In a large bowl, beat the cold heavy cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla extract until stiff peaks form
    15. Gently fold in the cold sour cream until smooth and thick
    16. Pulse the saved baked cake scraps in a food processor until fine crums form
    17. Place a honey cake layer on a serving plate and spread roughly 1/2 cup of frosting over top
    18. Repeat with all 8 layers, stacking them evenly
    19. Frost the top and sides of the cake with the remaining cream
    20. Coat the entire top and sides of the cake with the reserved cake crumbs
    21. Cover loosely and refrigerate for at least 8-12 hours before slicing
