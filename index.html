White Forest Cake

The white forest is a really appealing cake and fits its name. It's coated with white fudge and has fruits as decor. Apart from that, the taste is heavenly when baked well

Ingredients

  • Egg whites
  • Granulated Sugar (for meringue)
  • Egg yolks
  • All-purpose flour(sifted)
  • Whole Milk
  • Vanilla Bean/Extract
  • Cornstarch
  • Sheet Gelatin (softened in cold water)
  • White Chocolate(chopped)
  • Mascarpone Cheese
  • Heavy Whipping Cream(chilled)
  • Pitted Cherries in Brandy/Syrup
  • Whole Glacé/Fresh Cherries

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C and grease a deep cake pan
  2. Beat egg whites with sugar until firm peaks form
  3. Fold yolks into the meringue, then gently fold in the sifted flour
  4. Transfer to the prepared pan and bake for 45 minutes. Let cool completely
  5. In a hot skillet, saute pitted cherries with sugar
  6. Deglaze with cherry liqueur, add Kirsch/brandy, and carefully ignite to flambe
  7. Remove from heat and drain, reserving the juices for the soaking syrup
  8. Heat whole milk, vanilla, and 1/3 of the sugar in a saucepan
  9. In a bowl, whisk remaining sugar, egg yolks, flour, and cornstarch
  10. Temper the yolk mixture with hot milk, return to saucepan, bring to a boil, and cook for 2 minutes while whisking constantly
  11. Remove from heat and stir in softened gelatin, white chocolate, and mascarpone until smooth. Spread on a plastic-wrapped tray and chill
  12. Whip cold heavy cream to medium-firm peaks. Smooth out the chilled custard and gently fold in the whipped cream
  13. Boil water and sugar together in a small pot for syrup
  14. Remove from heat and mix in the cherry liqueur, brandy, and reserved juices from the flambeed cherries
  15. Place the bottom sponge layer down and generously brush with the cherry syrup
  16. Spread a layer of white chocolate diplomat cream and dot with flambeed cherries
  17. Repeat process for all layers
  18. Coat the entire cake with remaining diplomat cream
  19. Melt white chocolate to form decorative bark or shave it into curls, pressing shavings all around the cake

Prep time: 25-30 mins

Bake time: 45 mins

Vanilla Swiss Cake Roll

Ultra-soft, melt-in-your-mouth vanilla chiffon cake roll filled with a smooth mascarpone whipped cream filling that rolls perfectly without cracking or peeling

Ingredients

For the Chiffon Cake Batter
  • Cooking oil(neutral): 60g/4.5 tbsp
  • Milk: 80g/1/3 cup
  • Low-gluten/cake flour(sifted): 100g/3/4cup
  • Eggs(large and seperated): 6 whole
  • Vanilla Extract: 1/4 tsp
  • Lemon Juice: 2g
  • Granulated sugar: 65g/5 tbsp
For the Mascarpone Cream Filling
  • Mascarpone Cheese: 100g
  • Granulated Sugar: 18g/1.5 tbsp
  • Vanilla Extract: 1/4 tsp
  • Heavy Cream/Whipping Cream: 120g /1/2 cup

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together 60g cooking oil and 80g milk until combined
  2. Sift in 100g low-gluten flour and whisk until smooth
  3. Seperate 6 eggs then add the egg yolks and 1/4 tsp vanilla extract to the batter, mixing well until smooth and glossy
  4. Add 2g lemon juice to the 6 egg whites
  5. Whip on medium-high speed until large bubbles form, then add 1/3 of the suggar(about 22g)
  6. Continue whipping until fine bubbles form, then add another 1/3 of the sugar
  7. when soft lines appear, add the remaining sugar, reduce speed to medium-low, and whip until medium-to-wet peaks form
  8. Fold 1/3 of the meringue into the yolk batter until incorporated
  9. Pour the batter back into the remaining meringue and fold gently withh a spatula until smooth and uniform
  10. Pour the batter into a 25times40 cm baking pan lined with parchment paper
  11. Smooth the surface using a spatula
  12. Bake tat 340°F for 35 minutes until lightly golden
  13. Remove from the oven and immediately cover the top with a piece of parchment paper to trap moisture and prevent drying
  14. In a bowl, blend 100g mascarpone cheese, 18g sugar, and 1/4 tsp vanilla extract until smooth
  15. Gradually pour in 120g heavy cream and whip until smooth, thick and spreadable
  16. Flip the cake sheet over and peel off the bottom baking liner
  17. Trim off three outer edges. slice one short edge at a 45 degree angle,and make 4 shallow parallel cuts across the opposite short edge
  18. Spread the mascarpone cream evenly across the top surface
  19. Using the parchment paper underneath and a rolling pin for leverage, tightly roll the cake starting from the scored end toward the angled end
  20. Wrap the roll firmly in parchment paper and refrigerate for 1 hour to set
  21. Unwrap, slice with a knife and serve

Prep time: 25 minutes

Bake time: 35 minutes

Princess Cake

A stunning celebration cake layered with delicate sponge, raspberry jam, rich pastry cream and a high dome of whipped cream, encased in a smooth blanket of green marzipan

Ingredients

For the sponge base
  • Eggs(large, room temperature): 4 whole
  • Granulated Sugar: 3/4 cup
  • All-purpose Flour: 1/2 cup
  • Potato Starch or cornstarch: 1/2 cup
  • baking powder: 1 tsp
  • Salt: 1/4 tsp
For the custard and cream fillings
  • Egg yolks(4 large)
  • Granulated sugar: 1/3 cup
  • Cornstarch: 3tbsp
  • Whole Milk: 1 1/2 cups
  • Pure Vanilla Extract: 1 1/2tsp
  • heavy whipping cream(cold): 2 1/2 cup
For the marzipan covering & decoration
  • Marzipan: 14 oz
  • Green Food Coloring(gel)
  • Pink food coloring(gel)
  • powdered sugar

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a spring-form pan with parchment paper
  2. beat eggs and granulated sugar on high speed for 8-10 minutes until pale, thick and tripled in volume
  3. Sift in the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and salt. Gently fold together with a spatula
  4. Pour into the pan and bake for 20-25 minutes until golden. Let cool completely, then slice horizontally into 3 thin, even layers.
  5. In a saucepan, whisk egg yolks, sugar and cornstarch together. Gradually whisk in the milk
  6. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and comes to a gentle boil.
  7. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla extract, and strain into a bowl. Cover with plastic wrap directly touching the surface and chill completely
  8. Whip the cold heavy cream and powdered sugar until stiff, firm peaks form. Fold 1/3 of the whipped cream into the cooled vanilla pastry cream to lighten it
  9. Tint most of the marzipan with green food coloring(reserve a small piece to tint pink for a decorative rose)
  10. Roll the green marzipan out on a surface dusted with powdered sugar into a large circle
  11. Place the bottom sponge layer on a serving plate. Spread a thin layer of pastry cream over it, followed by the seedless raspberry jam
  12. Top with the second sponge layer. Spread the remaining vanilla pastry cream over top
  13. Heap the remaining plain whipped cream onto the middle, using an offset spatula to smoothen it into a tall smooth dome shape
  14. Place the third sponge layer gently over the top of the cream, pressing the edges slightly down to match the dome shape
  15. Gently lift the rolled green marzipan circle and drape it over the entire cake
  16. Smooth the marzipan down the sides, trimming away the excess around the base
  17. Dust the top lightly with powdered sugar, then decorate with a handmade pink marzipan rose in the center
    18. \
  18. Chill for at least 4 hours before serving to let the layers set

Prep time: 1 hour 15 minutes

Bake time: 20-25 minutes

Oreo Cake

a decadent layer cake made with rich, moist dark chocolate cake sponge filled and frosted with a fluffy Oreo cakes and cream buttercream

Ingredients

For moist chocolate cake
  • All-purpose flour: 2 cups
  • Granulated sugar: 2 cups
  • Unsweetened dark cocoa powder(dutch-processed): 3/4 cup
  • Baking powder: 2 tsp
  • baking soda: 1 1/2 tsp
  • salt: 1 tsp
  • Eggs(large, room temperature): 2 whole
  • Whole milk: 1 cup
  • Vegetable oil: 1/2 cup
  • Hot freshly brewed coffee or boiling water: 1 cup
  • Pure vanilla extract: 2 tsp
For the oreo buttercream
  • Unsalted butter(softened, room temperature): 1 1/2 cups
  • powdered sugar(sifted)
  • Heavy cream or milk
  • Pure Vanilla Extract: 1 tsp
  • Oreo Cookies(finely crushed): 1 1/2 cups
For decoration
  • Whole & halved oreo cookies
  • coarsely chopped oreos

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F
  2. Grease two cake pans and line the bottoms with parchement circle papers
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, dark cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt
  4. Add the eggs, milk, vegetable oil and vanilla extract. Beat on medium speed for about 2 minutes until smooth
  5. Stir in the hot coffee or boiling water using a spatula(the batter will be thin, which creates a moist cake)
  6. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared cake pans
  7. Bake at 350°F for 30 to 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean
  8. Cool in the pans on wire racks for 10 minutes, then remove from pans and let cool completely
  9. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter on medium-high speed for 3 minutes until pale and creamy
  10. Gradually add the powdered sugar, heavy cream, and vanilla extract. Beat until smooth and fluffy
  11. Gently fold in finely crushed orea crumbs until evenly distributed
  12. Place the first chocolate cake layer on a serving plate. Spread a thick layer of oreo buttercream over top
  13. Place the second cake layer on top. Frost the top and outer sides of the cake with the remaining buttercream
  14. Pipe buttercream swirls around the top border using a star tip, and place the whole or halved oreo on each swirl
  15. Press coarsely chopped oreos around the bottom border of the cake
  16. Chill for 30 minutes before slicing to set the frosting

Prep time: 35 minutes

Bake time: 30-35 minutes
